About Us

No Commerce Student
Should Feel Lost

Thulumbu was born from a deeply personal experience. Our founder, Abhirami Santhosh, a first-generation commerce student herself, once stood exactly where you stand today. She was passionate about CA and CMA but completely unaware of where to begin. Even growing up in Chennai, navigating the course structure was a struggle. That made her wonder: What about students from smaller towns across Tamil Nadu?

That question, along with eight years of teaching over 500 students, led to the creation of Thulumbu, a Tamil-first learning community built to make professional commerce education accessible, clear and exam-focused.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you book your free strategy call.

Are the classes taught in Tamil?

Yes. Thulumbu is a Tamil-first learning community — concepts are explained in Tamil so you understand difficult CMA topics faster, while technical terms stay in English.

Which CMA papers does Thulumbu coach?

We currently go deep on CMA Final — Paper 14 (Strategic Financial Management / SFM) and Paper 15 (Taxation). CMA Foundation and Intermediate are coming soon.

Who teaches the classes?

Taxation (Paper 15) is taught by our founder Abhirami — CA, CMA & CS qualified with 8+ years of teaching experience. SFM (Paper 14) is taught by Harish, a CA & CMA with a proven track record of results.

Are the classes live or recorded?

Classes are live and interactive. Live-batch students also get recorded access, so you can revise anytime — ideal if you are working or have a busy schedule.

What is included with enrolment?

Live classes, recorded access, study material & workbook, mock tests with answer evaluation, exam-writing techniques, a personalised timetable, weekend practice, and last-30-days intensive tracking.

How do I get started?

Book a free strategy call. We will understand where you are losing marks and give you a personalised, exam-focused plan — whether you enrol or not.
